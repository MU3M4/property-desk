Built-in rules

  • No source, no listing. Records without a source and verification date are rejected.
  • Flagged when old. Listings not verified in 30 days show a warning. Re-check price and availability before quoting.
  • Consent first. A lead cannot be saved without the consent box. Delete removes the lead completely.
  • Business contacts only. Save phone numbers that agents and developers publish for enquiries.
  • Photos by link. Use only images you have permission to show.
  • Honest empties. If nothing matches, the screen says so.
  • Local storage. Data stays in this browser only and is not shared or backed up. This is a prototype: have an advocate review ODPC registration and privacy notices before real use.
