ODPC Privacy & Right to Erasure

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, data subjects have the right to erase their personal records. This action immediately wipes all locally stored leads and custom listings from this browser.

Built-in Operational Rules & Compliance

  • No source, no listing. Every record requires an explicit source reference and verification date. Missing entries are rejected.
  • Flagged when old. Listings older than 30 days display a yellow warning: "Verified X days ago: recheck before quoting".
  • Consent-first CRM. Leads cannot be saved without ticking the consent checkbox.
  • Smart currency normalization. Prices given in millions (e.g. 15.5M), thousands (85K), or ranges are parsed accurately.
  • Automated 1-click WhatsApp quotes. Matched properties can be shared directly with clients via pre-filled WhatsApp links.
  • Honest empties. If no verified listings match a client's brief, the system states so clearly rather than hallucinating properties.
  • Local control & privacy. Your client database stays in this browser's local storage and is not shared with third parties.
